Resale certificate / CO requirement

Partly verified
Resale requirementFire Prevention Bureau inspection for sales of 1–2 family homes. The bureau notes extinguishers are no longer required for 1–2 family sales or rentals.
Who issues itCity of Cape May Bureau of Fire Prevention, 712 Washington St; 609-884-9599
FeeNot confirmed (the city's Sale of Property form could not be loaded)
  • Bureau page: "Fire Extinguishers are no longer required in One- or Two Family Dwellings (For Sales and/or Rentals)."

Flood zone

Flood zones are set property by property, not town-wide, so look up your exact address. Since March 2024 every NJ seller must answer the flood-risk questions on the disclosure form. See disclosures.
