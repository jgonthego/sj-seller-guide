Selling a house in Dennis Township, NJ

Town-specific resale certificate rules, property tax data and flood look-up for Dennis Township. For statewide rules (transfer fee, disclosures, attorney review) see the guides linked below.

VerifiedCensus GEOID 3400917560

Resale certificate / CO requirement

Verified
Resale requirementFire safety inspection (smoke/CO) required on sale before transfer. No resale CO found.
Who issues itDennis Township Fire Prevention / Bureau of Fire Safety, 571 Petersburg Rd, Dennisville
Fee$100 (>10 business days), $150 (4–10 days), $200 (≤4 days); $500–$2,000 penalty if transferred uninspected
Sources last checked 2026-10-17
  • Dennis Twp Fire Safety Inspection Application for sale of property (PDF, 2025)

Fees and procedures change. Confirm with the town before you schedule your closing.

Flood zone

Flood zones are set property by property, not town-wide, so look up your exact address. Since March 2024 every NJ seller must answer the flood-risk questions on the disclosure form. See disclosures.
