Selling a house in Wildwood City, NJ

Town-specific resale certificate rules, property tax data and flood look-up for Wildwood City. For statewide rules (transfer fee, disclosures, attorney review) see the guides linked below.

Resale certificate / CO requirement

Verified
Resale requirementCombined Transfer of Ownership application: Continued Occupancy (CCO), smoke/CO certificate, zoning certification and (except single-family) status of fire code
Who issues itCity of Wildwood (Construction Office / Fire Department)
FeeCCO $95 (1–5 units); smoke/CO $45 / $90 / $161 per unit (≥10, 4–10, <4 business days); zoning cert $50 / $70 / $125; status of fire code $25
Sources last checked 2026-10-17
  • City of Wildwood Application for Transfer of Ownership (PDF)

Fees and procedures change. Confirm with the town before you schedule your closing.

Flood zone

Flood zones are set property by property, not town-wide, so look up your exact address. Since March 2024 every NJ seller must answer the flood-risk questions on the disclosure form. See disclosures.
