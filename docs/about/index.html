Method, coverage & sources

This guide is built to be checked. Nothing town-specific is published without an official source link and a check date.

How towns are verified

  • Verified: the resale CO/inspection requirement and the issuing office confirmed from the town's own website, its code (eCode360/General Code) or an official application form.
  • Partly verified: the requirement is sourced, but the fee or another detail isn't published or conflicts between sources.
  • Not yet verified: no official confirmation yet. The page says "Not yet verified — check with the municipal clerk" and links to the town.
  • Search-engine summaries, real-estate blogs and third-party lists are never used as sources.

Data sources

  • US Census Bureau 2020 county subdivision list (NJ): the municipality list (Pine Valley merged into Pine Hill in 2022, giving 201 towns).
  • NJ Division of Taxation: 2025 General & Effective Tax Rates (PDF) and NJ Division of Taxation: County Equalization Tables: tax rates and equalization ratios.
  • NJ.gov: County and Municipal Web Sites directory: official municipal website links.
  • NJ DCA Division of Fire Safety: Fire Code Enforcement Directory (PDF, dated June 2021): historical reference only (2021).
  • FEMA Flood Map Service Center: flood maps.
